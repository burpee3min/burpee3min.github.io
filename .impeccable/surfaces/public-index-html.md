# Surface: intro (first screen of public/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: anyone with a phone camera (gym members, home, team events). Action: tap START and go to setup. Success: they start a run and later post the result card to Instagram/Reels.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first screen is the gym's wall-mounted LED interval clock itself, showing 3:00 and 000 reps and running its own demo, not a hero banner about a workout app. Refuses the athlete-photo + headline + feature-cards fitness landing.

OWN-WORLD: Matte black acrylic clock case on a rubber-floor charcoal ground; red 7-segment digits (DSEG7) with dim unlit ghost segments; white case-print labels in small caps; one physical red push button. Everything sits on an 8px LED dot grid; hierarchy only by digit size. States are printed on the display (READY, GO), never as badges. Rankings are LED rows of identical size.

STORY: Visitor sees a real competition clock ticking a demo (time running down, reps ticking up), understands "3 minutes, the camera counts reps", sees today's real top scores, presses START.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title "버피 3분 챌린지" printed on the case top. Clock panel fills most of the width: 3:00 huge, REPS 000 below. Directly under the case, a full-width red START push button, then a quiet 랭킹 보기 link. Top-3 LED leaderboard rows begin at the fold.

FORM: Gym interval timer (candidate 7 of 7, seed 3244cff5). Signature interaction: attract-loop demo on the clock; START press lights the button and flashes GO on the display before moving on. Raises: terminal (states printed on the display), kiln (rankings as same-size rows), azulejo (8px dot-grid alignment), zine (hierarchy by size only).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
